How to read it

Six lines, six distances

Under the stones sits a field. A white stone holds its point at +1, a black stone at −1, and every empty point settles to nine-tenths of the average of its neighbours. Influence fades with distance and pools where the surface gives it nowhere to leak.

Each line of a point's hexagram is the sign of that field averaged over a ring at a growing distance. The first line is the point alone, the second its four neighbours, and so on outward. The sixth is the whole board, so every point shares it. White-leaning reads yang, black-leaning reads yin. The lower trigram is the point and what touches it; the upper trigram is its surroundings.

The bar beside each line shows how far the average sits from zero. The line closest to zero is outlined: it is the 变爻 in waiting, the scale at which this point is nearest to changing sides.

An empty board has no lines at all. A position in exact balance cannot form its top line. Both are drawn as dotted.

Lab notebook · 14 Nov 2026

What stands, what is provisional, what comes next

Established

  • On the torus every point has four neighbours, so corner, side and centre are the same place.
  • Corner and belly, 9 × 9: the same six stones read differently by surface.
  • Two walls, 9 × 9: on the plane each wall owns the strip behind it. On the cylinder and torus the two strips join into one contested band.

Provisional

  • The field is a damped harmonic relaxation with damping 0.9. It stands in for an engine's ownership estimate and has no reading of life and death.
  • The ring distances and which line gets which ring are choices. Rings replaced nested discs during the build: discs overlap, so their lines agreed by construction and a board showed only a handful of hexagrams. The same warning as the τ ordering applies: a pattern produced by the assignment proves nothing about the assignment.
  • White as yang is a convention. Swap it and every hexagram becomes its complement.
  • Changing the surface keeps every stone in place and does not re-judge captures until the next move.

Testable

  • Coherence: do readings change in connected patches from move to move, or scatter? The per-line flip bars and the move history are the first instrument for this.
  • Do the lower lines turn more often than the upper ones across a real game? If so, 爻位 as scale gives the same cascade that 爻位 as timescale gave.

Next

  • Replace the field with a real ownership estimate and replay recorded games as a score.
  • A 16 × 16 torus with dyadic radii, so the six lines become a sign-wavelet of the field.
  • The 4 × 4 × 4 three-torus, where each point is a hexagram and each of its six liberties is one 变爻.

Margin, 14 Nov: the edge behaves as 后天. The torus is the uncalibrated surface; the plane is the torus plus a fixed background that favours whoever sits near the boundary.
