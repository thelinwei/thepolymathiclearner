A book we already half know.

00 · Recognition

Not a blank slate.

The Yi is not an abandoned machine waiting for modern people to switch it back on. It is a living inheritance, carried unevenly but persistently through Chinese cultural memory.

阴阳 yinyang (polarity), 五行 wuxing (five phases), 八卦 bagua (eight trigrams) and 易 yi (change) survive not only as doctrines but as a grammar of relation: advance and retreat, fullness and emptiness, timing and position, generation and restraint, auspiciousness and danger. Their images circulate far beyond readers of the original text.

The comparison with the Bible or Qur’an is useful if kept civilizational rather than theological. The Yi occupied a different religious and doctrinal position, but it likewise became a reference complex through which generations discussed nature, government, ethics, the body, uncertainty and right action.4

01 · Two genealogies

Memory and history must be held together.

One account records how the tradition understood its authority. The other asks what texts, inscriptions and archaeology can presently demonstrate. Neither should erase the other.

The received genealogy · 传统记忆 chuantong jiyi, inherited memory

Culture heroes give change a lineage.

  1. 伏羲 Fuxi draws the trigrams.Cosmos becomes image.
  2. 文王 King Wen orders the hexagrams and their judgments.Image becomes situational and political counsel.
  3. 周公 Duke of Zhou supplies the line statements.Each situation acquires position and internal movement.
  4. 孔子 Confucius transmits or authors the Ten Wings.Divination becomes philosophy and moral cultivation.
The critical reconstruction · 历史考证 lishi kaozheng, historical verification

A composite text accumulates over centuries.

  1. Bronze Age practices provide several divinatory precursors.Oracle-bone pyromancy and milfoil divination are related but not identical.
  2. The Western Zhou core develops during the early first millennium BCE.Its exact compilation history remains debated.
  3. Warring States writings elaborate symbolic, cosmological and moral readings.The Ten Wings are later than their traditional attribution.
  4. By the mid-second century BCE, core and commentary form the canonised Yijing.The book is an archive of accretion, not one act of authorship.
The distinction matters. Traditional attribution tells us about cultural meaning and legitimacy. Critical chronology tells us about composition and transmission. We will name the register whenever they differ.
02 · Formation

How the Changes became the Changes.

The familiar unity of 阴阳、五行、八卦 — polarity, five phases, eight trigrams — is itself a historical achievement: heterogeneous systems gradually braided through interpretation, use and institutional power.

Before the bookLate Shang

Divination as state technology

Inscribed bones and shells record organised inquiries about harvests, warfare, weather, illness, sacrifice and royal action. Cracks were produced, read and sometimes verified. This was institutional knowledge-making under uncertainty—but not yet the Zhouyi.

Early 1st millennium BCEWestern Zhou

A finite grammar of situations

The Zhouyi core associates sixty-four six-line figures with compressed judgments and line statements. A rule-bound operation selects a figure; interpretation joins image, precedent and circumstance.2

770–221 BCESpring & Autumn · Warring States

Prognostication becomes philosophy

Political narratives and later commentaries draw moral, symbolic and cosmological arguments from the figures. The manual becomes a book of wisdom without ceasing to be divinatory.

206 BCE–220 CEHan

Correlative synthesis

Yin–yang, seasons, directions, qi, calendrical cycles and five-phase classifications are increasingly coordinated with hexagram systems. The braid becomes a language for medicine, governance and cosmic order.6

960–1279 CESong and after

Diagrams organise a cosmos

Images, numbers and sequences are systematised with new force. Arrangements later called 先天 xiantian (prior heaven) and 后天 houtian (later heaven) become culturally authoritative even as legendary attribution and demonstrable history remain different questions.8

阴阳

Relational polarity

Contrast, dependence, alternation and reversal—not two isolated substances.

八卦 · 六十四卦

Configuration

A finite repertoire of differentiated situations, structured by position and relation.

五行

Transformation

Plural classifications of characteristic tendencies, relations and processes.

占筮 zhanshi

Situated judgment

A ritualised encounter among uncertainty, symbolic state, textual precedent and action.

What is the configuration?What is my position?What is changing?What is ripe?What conduct fits?
Our later distinction. Reading 八卦 as configuration and 五行 as transformation is productive—but it is our reconstruction, not the recovered blueprint of one original ancient system.
03 · The hinge

Before computers, there was procedure.

Divination was a pre-digital technology for thinking with uncertainty: a repeatable process of framing, constrained variation, symbolic encoding, retrieval and judgment.

“Computation” is an analogy with limits. The procedure transforms inputs into symbolically organised outputs. But ritual, authority, affect and practical wisdom are not reducible to an algorithm. The answer was never simply stored inside the cast.
04 · The digital turn

Not a superior picture. A new instrument.

The consequential difference is not that modern people can finally draw in three dimensions. Ancient diagrams were abstractions, not failed sculptures. The difference is computation through time.

We can now maintain continuous variables, update them simultaneously, run trajectories, compare counterfactuals, perturb initial conditions and make assumptions answerable to results. Advanced geometry and graph theory reveal relations among the sixty-four; dynamical systems ask what moves them; simulation allows the model to disagree with its makers.

Where AI enters. Not as a synthetic oracle. AI can traverse competing commentaries, expose modelling choices, generate counterexamples and help interrogate simulations. It remains collaborator and critic—not the source of legitimacy and never the bearer of responsibility.
No secret mathematics. This project does not argue that the Yi secretly contained hypercubes, differential equations or evolutionary dynamics. It asks what becomes visible when an inherited symbolic technology is reconstructed through contemporary formal and computational tools.
