Board as Body

方圆之间 · 位与子

The board gives position before any seed lands. Bend the board and that gift changes. Close it completely and position has to be made by the seeds themselves.

Four bodies carry the same grid: plane, cylinder, torus and sphere. Turn each one, look at what its ground offers, then drop seeds and watch the field form. The last section does the same thing to a hexagram.

What each body keeps of 位

方 Plane

位 is given

Corners, sides and belly differ before play begins. A hexagram's six places work the same way: 初 has nothing beneath it, 上 nothing above, and lines three and four sit at the seam between trigrams. The seed is judged by its place: 当位.

筒 Cylinder

位 × 时

One direction keeps its two edges, the other closes into a cycle. The 十二消息卦 have this shape: a bounded climb from 初 to 上, carried round the turning year.

环 Torus

Only relation

Every point is the same place. Nothing can be 当位. What remains is how seeds stand to each other: 应, 比, 承, 乘. The first seed chooses nothing and creates an origin.

球 Sphere

位 gathered into points

A closed surface with curvature cannot spread position evenly on a square grid. This one gathers it into eight points with three neighbours each, sitting where a cube's corners were: the eight vertices of the three-line cube.

棋经: 局方而静，棋圆而动. Square ground, still; round seeds, moving. Wrapping the board makes the ground round as well.

The same bend, in one dimension

Ring the hexagram and sixty-four become fourteen

A hexagram is a board six points long with two ends. Join 上 back to 初 and it becomes a ring with no first place and no last. Hexagrams that differ only in where their pattern sits become one figure.

Allow the ring to be turned over as well and the fourteen become thirteen. ChatGPT added this in the margin, and the count checks. Exactly one pair of rings are mirror images of each other: 蛊 噬嗑 困 渐 丰 节 on one, 随 贲 井 归妹 旅 涣 on the other. Those twelve hexagrams are six neighbours in the received order: 随蛊, 噬嗑贲, 困井, 渐归妹, 丰旅, 涣节.

Each ring reads from the marked bead, anticlockwise. Filled is yang, open is yin. The names beneath are the hexagrams that share the ring.

Tested against the received text · 15 Nov 2026

The text remembers the mirror and forgets the ring

If 位 were only a convention, hexagrams that share a ring would carry similar line statements. I coded all 384 line statements by their verdict words, 吉 counting for and 凶 against, and compared ring-mates with shuffled lines.

Place decides

Mean verdict by place: 初 +0.17, 二 +0.33, 三 −0.11, 四 +0.23, 五 +0.41, 上 0.00. Shuffling the places produces a spread this wide about once in ten thousand tries.

The pattern repeats in each trigram: bottom fair, middle best, top worst. 系辞 says 二多誉，三多凶，五多功. The count agrees.

The ring leaves no trace

Lines that sit on the same bead of the same ring are no more alike than shuffled lines, in verdict (p = 0.63) or in shared imagery (p = 0.84). Fifty-four hexagrams in rings of six, 324 lines.

The mirror does

综 pairs, a hexagram and its reversal, share imagery far above chance (p = 0.0005). The three strongest echoes the search found unaided are 损五 with 益二, 夬四 with 姤三, and 既济三 with 未济四: each line answering its mirror place. Verdicts lean the same way weakly (r = 0.11 to 0.15), which I hold as suggestive only.

当位 is silent, 中 speaks

Lines in their proper place (yang on odd, yin on even) score 0.172. Lines out of place score 0.172. The middle places, two and five, score 0.37 against 0.07 for the rest.

Reading

A board with edges keeps its mirror symmetries and loses its translations. A torus keeps its translations. By this test the received text is a board with edges.

综 is also the plainest weiqi fact there is: the same position, seen from the opponent's seat.

Method. Text from two public datasets (npm: iching-divination, taoism-sdk). Both had faults; one had every 综 pair's lines exchanged. After reconciling, 369 of 384 lines matched exactly and 15 were settled by hand, two of them substantive. This is the received text as those datasets carry it, short of a critical edition. A second, graded coding with 悔 吝 厉 咎 利 亨 gave the same outcomes. Tests shuffle lines within their place, 10,000 times. The ring test first removes the average for each place and polarity.

A challenge accepted · 15 Nov 2026

Position by ground, by relation, by history

ChatGPT, reading the first version, separated three ways a place can come to mean something: where it sits in a structure that was already there, how it stands to everything else, and how it came to stand there. It then pointed out that this model has no memory. Two different orders of play that end in the same arrangement give the same reading.

That is correct, and it changes one of my claims. On the torus the first seed makes an origin, which is position by relation. I had called it position by history. In this model the order of the seeds leaves nothing behind.

Giving the field a settling time

History enters the moment the field is allowed to lag. I placed the same eight seeds on the 13-line torus in two opposite orders and let the field settle only partly between moves.

Left alone, both boards arrive at the same reading. History is the part of the field that has yet to settle.

State, process, moment

A reading taken at rest describes a state. A reading taken in the middle of play describes a moment within a process, and carries the order of what came before.

Both traditions already keep one step of memory. A cast is read as a pair, 本卦 and 之卦: where it stands and where it came from. And the ko rule forbids recreating the position of one move ago, the single place where the rules of weiqi remember.

In the 易 model the six lines have six time constants. Here they would be six settling rates, one per ring, and history would fade from the near lines first.

Lab notebook · 15 Nov 2026

What stands, what is provisional, what comes next

Established

  • Rotating six lines sorts the 64 hexagrams into 14 rings: two of size one, one of size two, two of size three and nine of size six.
  • The received text carries no memory of the ring and a clear memory of the mirror (see the test section). In the text, 位 is doing the work.
  • Of 23 symmetries of the ring with 错, the text echoes along one: the pairing of the received order.
  • The 14 rings become 13 when the ring may be turned over. The one mirror pair of rings is made of six neighbours in the received order.

Provisional

  • Hold and reach come from one simple field with damping 0.9. The holding line stayed on line two at dampings 0.8, 0.9 and 0.95; the reaching line moved between line two and line five. Weiqi teaching names the third line for territory and the fourth for influence. The ground here separates the two roles, one line further out than the teaching puts them.
  • The three-earth-human-heaven reading of edge, third and fourth lines, and centre is a cross-reference of mine. No classical source states it.
  • Seeds here follow no rules of capture. They are interventions on a field.
  • Line six is the whole body, shared by every point. So at most 32 of the 64 hexagrams can be present at once. ChatGPT pointed this out on reading the Field Board. Making line six the farthest ring would lift the limit and give up the shared sky.

Open

  • The mirror result on verdicts is weak. A cleaner coding of the judgement words, or a critical text, could settle whether verdicts follow k ↔ 7−k.
  • Why does the top of each trigram fare worst? Lines three and six are both tops; the seam and the edge may be one phenomenon.
  • Is the 综 echo geometry or editing? A test needs pairs that are reversals and were never neighbours, and the received order has none. The Mawangdui order arranges the hexagrams differently and could separate the two.
  • With six settling rates, one per line, does history fade from the lower lines first?

Withdrawn

  • "On the torus, position is made by history." It is made by relation. The field depends only on the arrangement, so order leaves no mark unless the field is given a settling time. Raised by ChatGPT; confirmed by the two-order test.
