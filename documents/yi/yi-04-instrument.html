Plate 02The 木 frontier

Freedom under constraint is bought with foresight — up to a point.

木 was redefined in this pass (Plate 04 tells that story). It now amplifies whatever motion already exists, but only near a threshold: it does not choose a direction, it removes the friction at the moment of decision. That single change made 木 the operator that decides how escapable a context is — and it comes with a bill.

Holding 后天 pull at 0.10 and sweeping 木 across its range while the other four phases share the remainder:

The shape is the finding. This is not a smooth trade of foresight for freedom — it is a peak. Above it you lose both at once, because 木 only removes friction; it never drives, so a mix dominated by 木 crowds out the operators that actually displace you.

But the table above is a lucky case, and something wrong was said about it. The claim was that the peak sits exactly where foresight begins to fail. Across 26 randomised configurations it does not: the horizon typically breaks at 木 ≈ 0.06 while reach peaks at ≈ 0.14. The break comes first. Which means that in the general case there is no free lunch and no tidy coincidence — there are two different destinations, and you have to choose:

The furthest you can see (木 at the break) or the furthest you can go (木 at the peak). Between the two you are covering more ground while losing the ability to say where you will end up. That is a harder and more honest reading than the one given earlier, and it is the version the robustness study supports.

Plate 04What changed

The honest diff from v0.2.

Fixed · the walls

v0.2 clamped each coordinate at ±1 and bounced with restitution −0.52. Combined with 火 feeding velocity back positively, the system had no guaranteed dissipation: it became a pinball, and most of the structure you were watching was the box, not the field. v0.3 keeps an unbounded internal coordinate and displays x = tanh(u). The cube walls are now asymptotes — never reached, never hit. Which is also the better idea: no variable is ever fully 阴 or fully 阳.

Cut · the clocks

木 and 火 both contained Math.sin(t·…) terms. Those are a metronome wearing a costume — they made the display look alive while adding no dynamics. Removed. 火 is now bounded gain on velocity, and 木 is redefined below.

New · 木 means something measurable

木 now acts on an ensemble: it pushes each of twenty-four parallel futures away from their common mean. That is what branching is, and it makes 木 a quantity rather than an adjective. Raise it and the futures fan out; raise 金 and they snap back onto poles. The instrument reports the consequence directly.

New · the reading has an expiry date

Every ~half-second the instrument integrates all twenty-four futures forward and finds the time at which a quarter of them have left the current sign-region. That time is the predictability horizon — and it is the single most useful number here. It says how long this reading is worth anything, in the reading's own units.

New · 爻位 as real timescales

In v0.2 all six coordinates moved at one rate, so “bottom line” and “top line” were decoration. Now each 爻 carries its own time constant, sluggish at the base and twitchy at the top. A low crossing is a regime change; a high one is often noise. Toggle it off to feel the difference.

New · 后天 finally has a velocity

The field is a moving target f(t) rather than a fixed context, so the instrument can compute d‖x−f‖/dt: is the situation coming toward you or leaving? Static, diurnal and monsoon clocks are provided — the tropical calibration argument, made operational.

Resolved · 木, on the third attempt

Last version 木 pushed an ensemble apart without moving its mean, so it was inert to the solver — it bought optionality, not distance. Two redefinitions were tried.

木 v2 pushed each line away from zero in the direction it already leaned, strongest near the threshold. This was worse than useless: it is a sign-preserving operator, so it locks the current configuration in. Reachability fell from 59 regions to 34, and prescriptions dropped 木 even lower than before. Resolving indecision in the direction of the existing lean turns out to be conservatism, not exploration.

木 v3 keys on velocity rather than position: near a threshold it amplifies whichever way the line is already moving. That one change is the largest single effect in the project. At pull 0.10, reachable regions went from 8 to 37; at pull 0.30, from 6 to 25. And 木's share of a winning prescription now rises with the strength of the field — from 0.14 at zero pull to 0.37 at 0.30. 木 is the operator that makes a context escapable.

Tested and dropped · staged schedules

Last version predicted that staged mixes would reach regions no constant mix could, and that the classical 生 order would be the right staging. Tested against controls — free two-stage, 克-ordered two-stage, 生-ordered three-stage, and reverse-ordered three-stage, 1,800 candidates each. The prediction failed. At pull 0.10 the families landed within one region of each other (const 10, free2 8, 生2 10, 克2 11, 生3 10, rev3 9), and 生-ordered staging showed no advantage over its reverse.

Staging is not doing nothing in principle — a three-stage 生 run did reach two regions the constant sweep missed. But the effect is inside the noise of the search, which is a polite way of saying it is not the effect predicted. The classical ordering gets no support from this model, and recording that is better than quietly moving on to the next idea.

New · change has exactly two sources

This is the finding nobody expected. Once the fake clocks were gone, the default behaviour was stasis: with a fixed context and a fixed mix, the state slides to a resting configuration and stops. Which is correct, and worth staring at — nothing in the geometry generates motion by itself.

So motion has to come from somewhere nameable, and in this model there are only two candidates: the field moves (the 后天 clocks) or the mix turns over (生/克 running). The pentagram switch in the 五行 panel is now on by default, and the circulation it produces is what you are watching. Turn both off and the instrument freezes on purpose. That is not a bug to fix; it is the model saying that a still life needs no explanation and a moving one does.

Experiment · a strong context overwrites you

Raise 后天 pull past about 0.20 and watch: the configuration locks onto the field's own sign-pattern and stays there no matter what the mix does. Below about 0.12 your mix decides your figure and the state flips between regions as the pentagram turns. The default sits at 0.10, on the responsive side of that edge. That transition is sharp enough to be worth a name — it is the difference between having a character and having a job.

Fixed · the projection lied

v0.2 hung eight mini-cubes on the eight vertices of the outer cube. But a trigram is an octant — a region — not a corner, so the fibre was pinned to the wrong object. v0.3 stops nesting altogether. Q₆ = Q₃ × Q₃, so it draws two cubes, and lets the 8×8 matrix carry the joint structure. Two honest projections beat one clever wrong one.

Fixed · the frame budget

getBoundingClientRect() and getComputedStyle() were being called once per projected point per frame — hundreds of forced layout reads a second — and three fifty-step futures were re-integrated every frame. All now cached or throttled. Kept exactly as they were: your static fallback and your visible error box. Those were the best-engineered parts of the file.

Established

Change has exactly two sources. With a fixed field and a fixed mix the system goes still. Motion comes either from the field moving or from the mix turning over. Nothing in the geometry generates it.

木 keyed on velocity makes contexts escapable. At pull 0.10, reachable regions go 8 → 37. The peak's location (木 ≈ 0.14, band 0.05–0.20) survived randomised states, fields, pulls and ±35% jitter on every coefficient across 46 configurations. Mean gain +19 regions.

Reachability collapses at the first increment of context — but only without 木. Suppressed-木: 64 → 15 → 8 → 6 across pull 0.00 / 0.05 / 0.15 / 0.30. Active-木: 45 → 36 → 25 → 24. The cliff is a property of the four-operator system.

The 木 dividend rises with context — 0.7× at zero pull, 4.6× at 0.30. 木 is a liability when unconstrained and decisive when bound.

Patience only helps the unconstrained. Tenfold search horizon takes a 木-less system from 40 regions to 64 at zero pull, and buys literally nothing at any pull above zero. 木 substitutes for time you do not have.

生/克 turnover ≈ 109 time units against prescription arrival times of 2–4. Holding a mix is cheap; reach is the constraint, not discipline.

Provisional

The two-destination reading. Generically the horizon breaks (木 ≈ 0.06) before reach peaks (木 ≈ 0.14), so “furthest you can see” and “furthest you can go” are different mixes. Solid across 26 configurations, but the break threshold is a judgement call — a stricter criterion moves 木_break, though it did not change the ordering in any run.

爻位 as time constants. The ordering is the claim and it behaves sensibly: low crossings re-found everything above them. The six numbers themselves are asserted and unfitted.

The five operator definitions. Chosen to be qualitatively distinguishable. 木 has now been rewritten twice, which should tell you how provisional the other four are.

Refuted

Staged schedules along the 生 order. Predicted to beat constant mixes; did not. Within one region of the 克 order, the reverse order, and a single held mix. The classical sequence gets no support from this model. Not refuted under all tests — a directed search on segment durations is still open — but refuted under this one.

“27 of 64 reachable at zero pull.” A statement about the search budget, not the system. The true figure is 64.

“The peak sits where foresight fails.” True of the default state, false in general.

“木 is uniformly good for reach.” Negative at zero pull: 45 regions against 64 without it.

木 as position-lean amplification. Sign-preserving, therefore conservative. Made things worse.
