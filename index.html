linwei · an interactive archive · research as speculative play
The
A visual learning archive where design, mathematics, geometry, speculative thought and AI meet — a record of learning by making. Abstractions are given forms that can be turned, tested and broken; computation is treated as a companion for inquiry rather than an oracle.
Why this archive exists

Every document here began as something not understood. Rather than reading a way toward comprehension, the method is to build: an abstraction is given a body that can be turned, tested and broken, and the understanding arrives through the handling. What accumulates is not a shelf of explanations but a set of instruments.

The register is closer to art than to engineering — research as speculative play. Nothing here is trying to become a product. The questions are chosen because they are strange enough to be worth living inside for a while: what mathematics other civilisations might have grown, what shapes know that proofs do not, and what a three-thousand-year-old book about change would look like if it could actually move.

The work is published while still in motion. 易 iching — a working model of change is the current strand, and its retractions and unresolved questions are left visible on purpose; an archive about learning should show the edge as the edge actually looks.

The I Ching calls itself a book about change, then holds itself perfectly still. Part One gives the notation back its motion: a 爻 yao — one line of the six — becomes a dial rather than a switch, six lines become six continuous numbers, and the sixty-four become sign-regions of a six-dimensional cube — drawn honestly as two cubes rather than nested boxes.

織 ZHĪ · LOOM · NEWEST
易 iching · The Loom · 織

The Sixty-Four
on a Torus

A torus has two independent circulations, and each trigram is a closed ring of eight — so the split that once forced two bodies becomes two ways of going around one. The thread was expected to become a knot. It becomes a shuttle: the lower trigram advances once around and never reverses while the upper runs back and forth across it, seven passes to every advance. The torus is not a doughnut. It is a loom.

In progress · the surface is settled, the ordering is still the open choice
灯 dēng the lantern for the distribution, 織 zhī the loom for the structure — the first pair in this hunt that are not rival solids, but one surface and one section through it.

Computed, not asserted
